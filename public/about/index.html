About Me

Hi, I am Kashish. I am a third year Computer Science student, based in Toronto, Canada. I am starting to build a habit of writing down what I learn as I learn it. I came across Simon Willison’s TIL, and inspired by that I am starting my own “Today I Learned” series where I’ll put up what I just figured out, and maybe you find them useful too :) and also I can look back at them later and remember what I learned and how far I have come.

These days I am trying to get out of my comfort zone (by doing couple of stuff) which they say “learn in public” so I put up videos on Linkedin every week, I started contributing to open source Kubernetes and now writing.
